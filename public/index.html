Who is Amari

I was the skinny kid
with accidental abs.

In 2021 I weighed 125 pounds. Little to no muscle. I had abs because there was nothing covering them. Five years later I'm 185, and I did it without a single supplement. This is what actually moved the needle.

01

Nutrition came first.

The number one thing I changed was food. I started prioritizing red meat. Steak, ground beef, all of it. I got my protein and creatine from the plate, not a tub.

02

I stopped doing high reps.

I spent months at 12, 15, 20 reps and stalled. Lowering the reps and raising the weight is what finally built muscle and strength. I was stronger than I thought.

03

The biggest roadblock wasn't in the gym.

It was porn, and the way I was spending my attention. Cutting it out is the reason I could stay consistent. I prioritize myself and my health now. The guide gets into it honestly.

A taste of the method

Three rules, free.
The rest is in the guide.

These are the ones I'd shout across the gym. The ones that took me years to figure out are behind the paywall.

Rule

Form over weight.

Without proper form you can't target the muscle, and you will hurt yourself. Perfect the movement, then add weight progressively.

Rule

Every muscle, twice a week.

I trained each muscle once a week for years and stalled. Hitting everything twice changed my size and strength more than anything else in the gym.

Rule

Hydrate before anything.

You sweat while you sleep. Start the day with water before coffee, before food, before the gym.
